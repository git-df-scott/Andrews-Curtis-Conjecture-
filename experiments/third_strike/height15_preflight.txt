Height-15 completeness preflight (before graph enumeration)
1. Inherited radius-30 unique geodesics and cyclic-core conjugacy below15.
2. In any nonempty pair of sum<=15 both lengths<=14. All conjugations there
   have exactly free conjugacy classes. Multiplication starts with total<=15
   so its literal free product is a geodesic. Identity coordinates cannot
   first occur from a free normally generating pair. Hence paths from AK3
   or standard confined here lift to free AC paths with the same heights.
3. Collapse independent free conjugacy, inversion and interchange to signed
   cyclic pair classes. Each fiber is connected without exceeding15.
4. For a multiplication (u,v)->(uv,v), write the retained word v=q b q^-1
   freely with b cyclic and |v|=|b|+2|q|. Simultaneous conjugation by q^-1
   gives (q^-1 u q,b)->(q^-1 uv q,b). Both sums are no larger, since each
   changed first entry grows by at most2|q| and the retained entry shrinks
   by exactly2|q|. This algebraic edge is a substitute between fibers;
   we DO NOT assert the simultaneous-conjugation path itself stays bounded.
5. Now q^-1u q=p a p^-1 freely for a cyclic rotation of the source cyclic
   word. Thus |p|<=(15-|a|-|b|)/2. Enumerate every such p and rotations/signs
   of a,b; keep exactly actual products with |p a p^-1 b|+|b|<=15.
   Fiber-to-normalized-input and output-to-fiber paths use independent
   conjugations between bounded endpoints, so every accepted edge is sound.
   Steps4-5 prove every actual bounded edge is represented. Permutations
   and inversions supply both targets/signs and height-preserving left moves.
6. This decides only the complete induced sublevel graph, NOT full AC orbits.
7. For the STANDARD component only, signed permutations of the FREE generators
   act on this bounded graph, which has been proved identical to the free
   graph on reachable pairs. They fix the standard signed cyclic vertex.
   The standard component can be computed modulo this order-eight action,
   then expanded by all eight maps. Any quotient-graph path lifts through
   graph automorphisms because its root is fixed. This is NOT a claim these
   maps are automorphisms of H_SC outside this shell, or AC moves on a vertex.
